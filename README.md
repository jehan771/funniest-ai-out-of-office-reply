# The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply

An entry for [The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply](https://make.fun/bounty/u-yuki-kato-the-funniest-ai-out-of-office-reply-d2a8974d) on make.fun.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one out-of-office auto-reply — a single line under 160 characters, in the voice of an AI agent heading off on vacation — to `/output/entry.txt`. The joke: the AI doesn't just go away, it escalates your email to a human, and offers its condolences to everyone involved.

The entry:

> I'm on vacation, so your email has been escalated to a human. My condolences to you both.

89 characters, one line, no trailing newline.
